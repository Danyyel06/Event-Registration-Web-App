The problem

SIWES is broken.
Everyone knows it.

Every year, thousands of LASU students navigate Nigeria's mandatory internship program through a fragmented, paper-based system that fails them, their supervisors, and industry partners.

❌
No intelligent matching
Students land in misaligned roles. Companies receive unqualified interns. No algorithm — just manual guesswork.
⚠️
No equity enforcement
High-CGPA students monopolize opportunities. Tier 2 and 3 students remain unplaced with no structural protection.
📋
Paper logbooks, zero tracking
Attendance is logged, not competency. Supervisors disengage. Problems surface only at end of internship.
🔗
Assessment misalignment
University and company criteria never meet. Students are evaluated on rubrics they were never shown.

How it works

From enrollment
to placement to assessment.

01
🧙
8-Step Onboarding Wizard

Students build a verified profile — Job Family, skills, projects, preferences — and receive an instant Fit Score before they even apply.

02
⚡
Skill Verification Tests

Adaptive MCQ, coding, and scenario-based tests validate self-assessed skills and surface genuine competency gaps with learning pathways.

03
🎯
Dual-Track Fair Matching

The WSM algorithm pairs students with internships. Equity Track enforces T1→T2→T3 interleaving so every student gets a chance.

04
📊
Weekly Pulse Dashboard

SMART micro-goals, pinned evidence, Sunday reflections, and supervisor endorsements replace the paper logbook. Real growth, tracked weekly.
